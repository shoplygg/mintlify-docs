A customer is one email address at one store. An address becomes a customer the first time it places an order, or when you add it yourself. Find them under **Audience**, then **Customers**.

A customer's page shows their orders, what they have spent, their balance and its history, and whether they are an affiliate.

## The customer panel

Every store has a panel for its buyers at `your-store.myshoply.gg/customer-panel`. A buyer signs in with a code sent to the email they bought with, and reads their own orders and what was delivered.

The panel is always at the store's myshoply.gg address, also for a store with a domain of its own.

## Store balance

A customer has a balance at your store, in the store's currency, which they can spend at checkout. A balance gets there three ways:

- You add to it, on the customer's page.
- You refund an order as store credit.
- An affiliate moves commission to it, where your affiliate programme allows that.

To spend a balance, the buyer proves the email is theirs with a code sent to it. Whether the checkout offers paying with balance at all is a switch under **Payment Methods**.

Adding to a balance is money leaving your store without a payment, so the store's owner is told by email each time it is done in the dashboard.

## Blacklisting a customer

**Blacklist Customer** on a customer's page stops them buying: their email, and if you choose the IP addresses their orders came from. See [fraud protection](/guides/fraud-protection).

A customer any rule refuses shows **Blacklisted**, whether the rule is on their email, on an address their orders came from, or on their email's domain. **Unblacklist Customer** takes off the rules made for them and their email's, and the rules on their addresses that were added by hand unless you switch that off. A rule that covers other buyers too, such as an email domain or an IP range, stays: the dialog names it, and you change it on the Blacklist.

## Exporting customers

**Export CSV** on **Customers** makes a spreadsheet of them, starting from the list's filters: when they were first seen, everyone or only affiliates, and the order. Each row is a customer: their email, when they were first seen, the orders they paid for and what they spent, their first and last paid order, their store balance, and their affiliate code and balance where they have one. Every customer that matches is in the file, and a store can export 30 files every 10 minutes with its other exports.
