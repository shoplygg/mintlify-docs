The [embed](/guides/embeds) opens the checkout over your page. The Checkout Kit puts it **in** your page, taken apart. The order can sit in your sidebar, the email box under a heading of yours, the ways to pay in your main column and the Pay button where your design ends. Seven pieces in all, each looking the way your site looks.

It needs product IDs and no key, and it sells a whole cart.

## Adding it

```html
<script src="https://shoply.gg/sdk/kit.js" defer></script>

<div data-shoply-piece="summary"></div>
<div data-shoply-piece="email"></div>
<div data-shoply-piece="payment"></div>
<div data-shoply-piece="pay"></div>

<script>
  addEventListener("DOMContentLoaded", function () {
    var kit = shoplyKit({ product: "PRODUCT_ID", variant: "VARIANT_ID" });

    kit.on("paid", function () {
      location.href = "/thank-you";
    });
  });
</script>
```

Or all of it in one place: `<div data-shoply-piece="checkout"></div>`.

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is |
| - | - |
| `checkout` | All of it, in one piece. |
| `payment` | The payment methods, and once Pay is pressed the payment itself and then the paid order. |
| `summary` | What is being bought, its quantity, and what it comes to. |
| `coupon` | The box a discount code goes in. |
| `email` | Where the order is sent. |
| `billing` | The billing address, for a method that asks for one. |
| `pay` | The Pay button. |

A kit has one of `checkout` and `payment`: that piece is the checkout itself, and the others are optional. A piece you leave out is drawn inside it, where the checkout would have it. The exceptions are `summary` and `coupon`, which are only where you put them.

## What it sells

```javascript
// One product
shoplyKit({ product: "PRODUCT_ID", variant: "VARIANT_ID", quantity: 1 });

// A cart, all from the one store
shoplyKit({
  items: [
    { product: "PRODUCT_ID", variant: "VARIANT_ID", quantity: 1 },
    { product: "ANOTHER_PRODUCT_ID" },
  ],
  coupon: "WELCOME10",
  email: "ada@example.com",
});
```

A line that names no variant is given its product's first. When your own basket changes, name the cart again: `kit.set({ items: [...] })`.

## How it looks

```javascript
shoplyKit({
  product: "PRODUCT_ID",
  theme: "auto", // "light", "dark" or "auto"
  look: {
    accent: "#12a37f", // the button, the chosen method. Your store's colour if left out.
    text: "#1d2430",
    muted: "#6c7480",
    field: "#ffffff", // what a box or a row is filled with
    line: "#d9d4c8", // its edge
    radius: 4,
    font: "Georgia, serif",
    css: ".shoply-kit-button { text-transform: uppercase }",
  },
});
```

`css` is rules of your own, for these classes and nothing else:

| Class | What it is |
| - | - |
| `.shoply-kit-field` | A box that is typed in or picked from. |
| `.shoply-kit-error` | The line under a box that was refused. |
| `.shoply-kit-method` | A payment method. `[aria-checked="true"]` is the one chosen. |
| `.shoply-kit-method-name` | Its name. |
| `.shoply-kit-button` | Pay. |
| `.shoply-kit-line` | A product in the order, with `-line-name` and `-line-price`. |
| `.shoply-kit-row` | A line of the totals. |
| `.shoply-kit-total` | The total. |
| `.shoply-kit-apply` | The coupon's Apply. |

With `:hover`, `:focus`, `:focus-within`, `:disabled`, `::placeholder` and `@media`. A rule may set how a thing looks: colours, borders, type, spacing, size, shadows, transitions. It may not fetch anything (no `url()`, no `@import`) or move things about (no `position`, `display` or `transform`). Whatever was left out of what you wrote is said in your page's console.

## From your own script

| Call | What it does |
| - | - |
| `kit.place("summary", "#order")` | Put a piece in an element, or move it there. |
| `kit.remove("summary")` | Take a piece away. |
| `kit.set({ items, quantity, coupon, theme, look })` | Change what it sells or how it looks. |
| `kit.pay()` | Press Pay from a button of your own. |
| `kit.on("ready", fn)` | The checkout is drawn. |
| `kit.on("change", fn)` | `fn({ state, total, currency, items, method, coupon })` |
| `kit.on("problem", fn)` | `fn({ code, message })`: something the buyer has to put right, or that went wrong. |
| `kit.on("paid", fn)` | The order is paid. |
| `kit.destroy()` | Take the whole kit off the page. |

`shoply:paid` is also sent on `window`, as the embed sends it.

<Warning>
  `paid` tells your **page**. It carries no order ID and is no proof of payment. Hand over goods of your own when your server receives the [`order.paid` webhook](/guides/webhooks).
</Warning>

## Things to know

- **Your terms are yours to show.** The kit asks no buyer to agree to your store's terms and shows no link to them, since it is on your own site. Put them on your own page.
- **The pieces run on `checkout.shoply.gg`**, in frames. Card details, the buyer's email and the order never touch your page.
- **Analytics** work as they do for the [embed](/guides/embeds#your-stores-analytics): load `shoply.js` on your pages and start it with your store's ID.
- Prices, the buyer's currency, coupons and stock are the same as on your Shoply store.
