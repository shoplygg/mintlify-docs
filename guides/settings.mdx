Settings opens over whatever page you are on. Press **Settings** in the dashboard's rail, pick a part from its list, and close it when you are done: the page behind it is as you left it. A change shows a bar at the foot of the window with **Save changes** and **Discard**.

## What is where

| Part | What it holds |
| - | - |
| **General**, **Branding**, **Business** | The store's name, description, ID and whether it is open; its logo, icon and accent colour; the business name and country that [Finance](/guides/finance) asks for. |
| **Domains** | The store's myshoply.gg address, and [a domain of your own](/guides/custom-domain). |
| **Statistics** | What buyers see about your stock, your sales and your [reviews](/guides/reviews). |
| **Links** | Where buyers go for help, and where they find you. |
| **Payments** | [How buyers can pay](/guides/payment-methods). |
| **Policies** | Your terms and your refund policy. |
| **Affiliates** | [The affiliate programme](/guides/affiliates) and its rates. |
| **Delivery emails** | What the email with a buyer's order says. |
| **First-order coupon** | A code for a buyer's next order. |
| **Notifications** | When Shoply tells you something, and where. |

The rest of this page is the four parts no other guide covers.

## Delivery emails

Every paid order sends its buyer one email with what they bought. Two things in it are yours.

| | |
| - | - |
| **Default instructions** | Shown in every delivery email, under whatever a variant's own instructions say. Use it for what is true of every order: where to ask for help, how long a key is good for. |
| **Buttons** | Up to four, each with its words and its link. A saved link from **Links** can be dropped into the link box. Drag a button by its grip to sort them, and switch one off to hide it without deleting it. |

**In the email**, under the buttons, draws them as the email will: the first filled across the width, the rest outlined in a row.

Two buttons are always added after yours and cannot be removed: **View your order**, which opens the buyer's [customer panel](/guides/customers-and-balance), and **Leave a review**, which opens the order's own page.

<Note>
  Delivery emails and invoices are always in English, whatever language your store and your buyer read.
</Note>

## First-order coupon

A thank-you for somebody's first order: a single-use code for their next one.

| Setting | What it does |
| - | - |
| **Send first-time buyers a coupon** | The switch for all of it. Off, nothing is sent. |
| **Discount** | How much the code takes off. |
| **Expires after** | In days. `0` means it never expires. |
| **In its own email** or **With the delivery email** | Whether the code arrives by itself after the order, or inside the email that delivers it. |

The preview beside the settings shows the email, or the part of the delivery email, as the buyer gets it. The code works once.

## Policies

**Terms of Service** and **Refund Policy** are yours to write. A buyer has to accept them at the checkout before they can pay, and both are pages of your store.

A policy you leave empty is not shown.

<Note>
  The [Checkout Kit](/guides/checkout-kit) shows no tick and no link to either: there the checkout is on your own page, and your terms are yours to show on it.
</Note>

## Notifications

Two ways of being told, each with its own switches.

### By email

| Setting | What it sends |
| - | - |
| **Email me on every sale** | The customer, the total and what they bought. Replying reaches the customer. |
| **Email me when stock runs low** | One email listing everything that ran low, once a variant is at **Warn me at** or fewer. |
| **Email me when an affiliate asks for a payout** | How much, and how they want to be paid. On for every store, and with no address typed it goes to your account's email. |
| **Send alerts to** | The address all three go to. |

**Send test**, beside the address, sends a sample delivery email there with a made-up product and key, the way a real one is sent. Nothing is added to your orders or your coupons. One every five minutes.

### On Discord

Switch **Notify me on Discord** on and paste a webhook URL from your server: **Server Settings**, then **Integrations**, then **Webhooks**. Then pick what is posted.

| Post about | What is posted |
| - | - |
| **New orders** | The buyer, the total and the delivered items. |
| **Low stock** | A variant at its own **Warn me at** or fewer. |
| **Errors** | Delivery emails that failed and orders that could not be fulfilled. |
| **New reviews** | The stars, the words, the customer and the order. Automatic reviews are not posted. |
| **Payout requests** | The affiliate, the amount and how they want to be paid. |

**Send test**, beside the webhook, posts a sample alert to the URL in the box, saved or not. Five in five minutes.

A webhook URL is kept encrypted and is never shown again: paste a new one to replace it, or press **Remove webhook**.

<Note>
  This is for alerts in a Discord channel. To have your own server told when something happens, use [webhooks](/guides/webhooks).
</Note>
