Upselling is under **Catalogue**, then **Upselling**. It has a tab for each place a buyer meets an offer, and figures over them for the period you pick beside the add button.

## Add-ons

An add-on is a tick on a product's page, under its options. Ticked, it goes into the cart with the product, and the buyer can still tick or untick it on the checkout.

| | |
| - | - |
| **What It Sells** | **A Plain Extra**: a name and a price, with nothing behind it to deliver. Its **Message After Payment** is on the buyer's receipt and order page. Or **One of Your Products**, at a **Price as an Add-on** of your own, delivered as that product always is. |
| **How It Is Offered** | **Optional**, which the buyer ticks. **Ticked**, which they can untick. **Included**, which is always in the order at its price. Buyers read "Included" in place of a price only where it costs nothing. |
| **Charged** | **Once per Order**, or **For Each Item** of the product it is beside. |
| **Offer It With** | Products, or whole categories, so that a product you add to one later has it too. |

A plain extra's **Cost Per Unit** counts toward your profit in [Finance](/guides/finance) and is never shown to buyers. Each add-on becomes a line of the order of its own, marked with the line it was ticked beside. One line takes up to 10.

## Cart offers

Under the cart's lines, the store offers up to three things at once. **Add Cart Offer** makes one of two kinds.

| | |
| - | - |
| **An Offer Beside It** | Another product, offered while the cart holds what **It Shows When** asks for: a product, a category, or a cart total. Taken, it is a line of one at the offer's price. |
| **An Upgrade** | A better product in place of one in the cart. **It Replaces** names the products it is offered on, and **Upgrade To** is what the buyer gets instead. Taken, it replaces that line, every item of it. |

Each has a **Price**: **Its Price**, **Percent Off** or **Amount Off**, on that order only. **Words Over It** is the line the store shows above the offer. Left empty, the storefront says "Goes well with it" or "Upgrade and save", in the buyer's language.

If what an offer asked for leaves the cart, the product it added stays there at its own price.

## At checkout

One offer, with a tick above **Pay** on Shoply's checkout and in the [Checkout Kit](/guides/checkout-kit). It is not in the [embed](/guides/embeds), which sells the one product it was opened on. It shows under the same conditions as a cart offer, and has two switches of its own, since the checkout is the one place that knows who is buying.

| | |
| - | - |
| **First Orders Only** | Offered only to a buyer who has never paid at your store. |
| **Hide When Already Bought** | Not offered to a buyer who has bought it before. |

Left empty, its words are "Add to Your Order", in the buyer's language.

## Bought together

Under **Buy now** on a product's page, up to four products a buyer can add with one press, each at its own price. Yours come first: **Add Bought Together** picks a **Product**, and the products it is **Shown Under**.

**Worked Out From Your Orders**, at the head of the tab, fills the rest with what your paid orders show is most often bought with that product. It is on until you switch it off.

## When an offer runs

**More Options**, at the foot of each dialog but Bought Together's, has **Starts** (**Now**, or **At a Set Time**) and **Ends** (**Never**, or **At a Set Time**). An offer's menu has **Turn Off**, which keeps it for later.

## The figures

| | |
| - | - |
| **Upsell Revenue** | What the offers sold on paid orders, at the price they were sold for. |
| **Orders With an Upsell** | Paid orders that had at least one. |
| **Times Shown** | How often a storefront or the checkout showed one. A robot's look is not counted. |
| **Taken** | Of the times shown, the share that went into a paid order. |

Each offer's row has the same figures for itself. A store holds up to 200 upsells of every kind together, and each names up to 100 products and categories.

## On your storefront

Every theme draws add-ons, the cart's offers and bought together. A storefront whose code you have changed in the code studio keeps its own files: it shows none of these until its files do. An add-on that is **Included** is in the order whatever the storefront shows, and the checkout lists it.

On a site of your own, the [storefront SDK](/sdk/overview) answers a product's add-ons and what is bought together with it, and has calls for the cart's offers.
