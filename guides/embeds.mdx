The embed opens the checkout over your own site. Your visitor presses a button on your page, the checkout opens in a card over it, and they pay without leaving.

It needs a product's ID and nothing else: no key, no server of your own.

## Adding it

Load the script once, and give any button the product it sells.

```html
<script src="https://shoply.gg/sdk/embed.js" defer></script>

<!-- The whole product: the buyer picks the option first. -->
<button data-shoply-product="PRODUCT_ID">Buy now</button>

<!-- One option: straight to paying. -->
<button data-shoply-product="PRODUCT_ID" data-shoply-variant="VARIANT_ID">Buy now</button>
```

The dashboard's **Developers**, then **Embeds**, page writes this for a product you pick, with its IDs filled in.

## What a button can say

| Attribute | What it does |
| - | - |
| `data-shoply-product` | The product. Needed. |
| `data-shoply-variant` | One option of it. Left out, the buyer picks. |
| `data-shoply-quantity` | How many to start with. |
| `data-shoply-coupon` | A coupon code to apply. |
| `data-shoply-theme` | `light`, `dark` or `auto`. The default is the buyer's own device. |
| `data-shoply-ref` | An affiliate's code. Not needed as a rule: a page opened through an affiliate's link is noticed by itself and remembered for 30 days. |

## Filling in what you already know

If your page already knows the buyer, it can fill the checkout's boxes in for them. Give the value, or with `-from` a selector for a field on your page to read when the button is pressed.

```html
<button
  data-shoply-product="PRODUCT_ID"
  data-shoply-email-from="#email"
  data-shoply-billing-name="Ada Lovelace"
  data-shoply-billing-country="GB">
  Buy now
</button>
```

The billing fields are `-name`, `-line1`, `-line2`, `-city`, `-state`, `-postal-code` and `-country` (two letters). The buyer sees what was filled in and can change any of it.

## From your own script

```javascript
shoplyEmbed.open({ product: "PRODUCT_ID", variant: "VARIANT_ID", theme: "dark" });

shoplyEmbed.open({
  product: "PRODUCT_ID",
  email: "ada@example.com",
  billing: { name: "Ada Lovelace", line1: "1 Example Street", city: "London", postalCode: "N1 9GU", country: "GB" },
});

shoplyEmbed.close();
```

Your page can hear what happened:

```javascript
window.addEventListener("shoply:paid", () => {
  // The order has been paid.
});

window.addEventListener("shoply:close", () => {
  // The checkout was closed.
});
```

<Warning>
  `shoply:paid` tells a page, and a page can be made to say anything. Hand over goods of your own when your server receives the [`order.paid` webhook](/guides/webhooks), not when a page says so.
</Warning>

## Your store's analytics

For an order to say where it came from, the page it was bought on has to be counted as a visit. Load the storefront script on your pages and start it with your store's ID, and the embed does the rest.

```html
<script src="https://shoply.gg/sdk/shoply.js"></script>
<script>shoply.init("YOUR_STORE_ID");</script>
```

## Things to know

- The checkout runs on `checkout.shoply.gg`, in a frame. Card details never touch your page.
- Prices, the buyer's currency, coupons and stock are the same as on your Shoply store.
- The first screen shows the product's picture, name and price, with **View Description** under the price where the product has one. It opens the description in a small panel, as plain text.
- A product whose options ask the buyer questions asks them on the first screen, under the options, for the option chosen, and **Continue** waits for the required ones. See [custom fields](/guides/custom-fields).
- The product's extras from [Upselling](/guides/upselling) are offered under it on the paying screen: one box with an extra's name, its price and **Add**. Where there are several, the name opens a list of them. An extra you tick for the buyer starts added, and one you include is listed with the total.
- The buyer's controls are at the top right: a menu with the look (light or dark) and the language in it, **Pay on My Phone**, and **Currency** where your store has adaptive pricing on. With it off there is one currency and nothing to pick. "Secure checkout by Shoply" is the last line at the foot.
- A buyer closes it by pressing anywhere on your page outside it, or with Escape. On a phone, where it covers the screen, it has a cross at the top right as well. Opened by itself, as **Pay on My Phone** opens it, there is nothing behind it to go back to.
- It has a thin outline, so a dark checkout does not run into a dark page behind it.
- An embed sells one product at a time. For a cart, or for a checkout that is part of your page instead of over it, use the [Checkout Kit](/guides/checkout-kit).
- A product that is private cannot be bought through the embed.
