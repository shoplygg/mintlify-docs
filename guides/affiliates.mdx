---
title: "Affiliates"
description: "Let your customers earn commission on what is bought through their link"
---

An affiliate is a customer of your store with a link of their own. When somebody buys through it, the affiliate earns a share of the order.

## Switching it on

The programme is off until you switch it on, in **Settings**, under **Affiliates**. There you set:

- The **commission** and the **buyer's discount** a new affiliate gets.
- Whether a customer may **join by themselves** from the customer panel.
- Whether an affiliate may **change the code** their link ends in.
- Whether they may **move commission to their store balance**, and whether they may **ask for a payout**.

While it is off, no link takes anything off or earns anything, and the customer panel says nothing of affiliates.

## How a link works

An affiliate's link is your store's address with their code: `?ref=ada`, `/ref/ada` and `/ref=ada` all work. A click counts for 30 days, and the last link clicked wins. A buyer who comes through a link gets the affiliate's discount, on top of any coupon.

## What an affiliate earns

Commission is a percent of what the buyer paid for the products: after every discount, before a payment method's fee and tax. It lands on the affiliate's balance when the order is paid.

- An affiliate earns nothing on their own orders, and gets no discount from their own link.
- When an order is refunded or charged back, its commission is taken back, as far as the affiliate's balance still covers it.
- A switched off affiliate earns nothing new, and keeps what they earned.

## Payouts

Shoply moves no money to affiliates. A payout is a request and a record: the affiliate says how they want to be paid, you pay them yourself, and press **Mark as Paid**, or decline with a reason they can read. The amount leaves their balance when they ask, and comes back if you decline.

You are told of a request by email, and in Discord if your store's alerts go there.

## With the API

See [Affiliates](/api-reference/affiliates/list-affiliates). The webhook events `affiliate.created`, `affiliate.payout_requested` and `affiliate.payout_answered` tell your server as it happens.
