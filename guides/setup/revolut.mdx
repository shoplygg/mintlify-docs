Revolut takes cards, Revolut Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay on its own page. You need a Revolut Business account with a Merchant account, which you apply for in Revolut Business.

## In Revolut Business

1. Once your Merchant account is approved, open **APIs**, then **Merchant API**, in your Revolut Business account.
2. Generate the **Production** API keys, and copy the **Secret** key. The public key beside it is not the one.

A sandbox account is made separately, through Revolut's sandbox sign-up, and is approved at once. Its **Merchant API** page has sandbox keys of its own, which work only in the sandbox.

There is nothing else to set up in Revolut: saving puts Shoply's webhook in your account.

## In Shoply

1. In your Shoply dashboard, open **Settings**, then **Payment Methods**.
2. Under **Add a Payment Method**, find **Revolut Business** and press **Connect**.
3. Paste the secret key into **Secret Key**.
4. Press **Test Connection** to try what you pasted, then **Connect App**.

Revolut keeps at most ten webhooks an account. If yours already has ten, saving says so: remove one you no longer use in Revolut and save again.

How Revolut Business works once it is connected, at the checkout and on an order, is in [Payment methods](/guides/payment-methods#revolut-business).
