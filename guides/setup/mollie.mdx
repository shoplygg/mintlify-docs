Mollie takes cards and the local methods of your buyers' countries, on its own page.

## In Mollie

1. In your Mollie dashboard, open **Developers**, then **API keys**. Only the Admin and Developer roles can open it.
2. Copy your live API key. It starts with `live_`. A key that starts with `test_` completes and delivers orders with no money moving.
3. Switch on at least one payment method in your Mollie dashboard's payment methods settings. Cards, Klarna and some others need Mollie to verify you first, and a live key works once Mollie has approved your website profile.

There is nothing else to set up in Mollie: each payment tells Mollie where to report.

## In Shoply

1. In your Shoply dashboard, open **Settings**, then **Payment Methods**.
2. Under **Add a Payment Method**, find **Mollie** and press **Connect**.
3. Paste the key into **API Key**.
4. Press **Test Connection** to try what you pasted, then **Connect App**.

Saving refuses a key Mollie does not know, and a profile with no payment method switched on. **Test Connection** says how many are on.

How Mollie works once it is connected, at the checkout and on an order, is in [Payment methods](/guides/payment-methods#mollie).
