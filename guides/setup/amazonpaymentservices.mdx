Amazon Payment Services (once PayFort) takes cards on its own page. Its accounts are opened through its sales team.

## In Amazon Payment Services

1. In your back office, open **Integration Settings**, then **Security Settings**. Copy the **Merchant Identifier**, the **Access Code**, the **SHA Request Phrase** and the **SHA Response Phrase**. Leave the **SHA Type** as it is: Shoply works out which one you chose.
2. Note the one currency your account charges in.

### The feedback addresses (optional)

With them, payments are confirmed sooner. Without them, Shoply still follows each payment, a little later.

1. Open Shoply's Amazon Payment Services dialog and copy the **Callback URL** it shows.
2. In your back office, open the **Technical Settings** tab and press **Add New Technical Settings**. Choose **Redirection** as the **Integration Type**, and press **Activate**.
3. Paste the Callback URL as both the **Notification URL** and the **Direct Transaction Feedback**.
4. Set **Send Response Parameters** to **Yes**, and press **Save Changes**.

Shoply sends the address a buyer comes back to with each payment, so what you put in **Redirection URL** is not used.

## In Shoply

1. In your Shoply dashboard, open **Settings**, then **Payment Methods**.
2. Under **Add a payment method**, find **Amazon Payment Services** and press **Connect**.
3. Paste the four values into the boxes of the same names, and type your account's currency into **Currency**, as three letters: `AED`, `SAR`, `USD`.
4. Press **Test Connection** to try what you pasted, then **Connect App**.

Saving checks them with Amazon Payment Services and refuses one it does not take, at its box. **Test Connection** says whether the account is a sandbox one, and which SHA type it uses. An account that charges only in KWD, QAR, BHD, OMR or JOD cannot be connected.

How Amazon Payment Services works once it is connected, at the checkout and on an order, is in [Payment methods](/guides/payment-methods#amazon-payment-services).
