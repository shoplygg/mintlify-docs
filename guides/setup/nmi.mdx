NMI is a gateway, sold under its own name and by many providers under theirs. Each has the same Merchant Portal. Shoply needs two security keys from it.

## In your gateway

1. In the Merchant Portal, open **Settings**, then **Security Keys**, and press **Add a New Key**. On an older portal it is under **Options**, **Settings**, **Security Keys**.
2. Make a private key: give it a name, pick the user it belongs to, and choose the **API** permission. If your portal lists **Query** on its own, choose it too.
3. Make a public key with the **Tokenization** permission. If you will switch on 3D Secure or Kount in Shoply, give it **Checkout** as well.

### The webhook (optional)

With it, a payment you void in your gateway refunds the order in Shoply too.

1. Open Shoply's NMI dialog and copy the **Callback URL** it shows.
2. In the Merchant Portal, open **Settings**, then **Webhooks**, and press **Create**. Paste the Callback URL and pick the transaction events.
3. The **Webhooks** settings page shows your signing key. Copy it.

## In Shoply

1. In your Shoply dashboard, open **Settings**, then **Payment Methods**.
2. Under **Add a payment method**, find **NMI** and press **Connect**.
3. Paste the public key into **Public Security Key** and the private key into **Private Security Key**.
4. If your provider gave you a gateway address of its own, paste it into **Gateway Address**. Leave it empty for NMI's own.
5. If you made the webhook, paste its signing key into **Webhook Signing Key**.
6. Press **Test Connection** to try what you pasted, then **Connect App**.

**Test Connection** asks the gateway whether it knows the private key. The public key can only be tried in a browser, so the first card window is its test. An account in the gateway's test mode approves every card and charges nothing, and Shoply cannot tell: switch it back before you sell.

How NMI works once it is connected, at the checkout and on an order, is in [Payment methods](/guides/payment-methods#nmi).
