Stripe takes card payments on your checkout, in Stripe's own card form. You need a Stripe account that can take live payments.

## In Stripe

### Your keys

1. Open [API keys](https://dashboard.stripe.com/apikeys) in your Stripe Dashboard, and make sure it shows **live mode**, not a sandbox.
2. Copy the **Publishable key**. It starts with `pk_live_`, and Stripe always shows it.
3. Get a secret key, one of two ways:
   - Beside the standard **Secret key**, press **Reveal live key** and copy it. It starts with `sk_live_`. Stripe reveals only a key it made for you.
   - Or press **Create restricted key** and give it only what Shoply uses: **Write** for Payment Intents and Refunds, and **Read** for Balance and Disputes. Leave the rest at **None**. It starts with `rk_live_`.

A live key you create yourself is shown once, as it is made. Copy it before you close Stripe's dialog.

### The webhook (optional)

Shoply asks Stripe about each payment while the buyer waits, so payments complete without a webhook. With one, a payment is confirmed even when the buyer closes the page the moment they pay, and disputes reach your orders sooner.

1. Open Shoply's Stripe dialog and copy the **Callback URL** it shows over **Webhook Signing Secret**.
2. In Stripe, open **Workbench**, then [Webhooks](https://dashboard.stripe.com/webhooks), and press **Create an event destination**. Choose **Your account**.
3. For the payload, choose **Snapshot**, not **Thin**. Shoply reads the whole event, which a thin one leaves out.
4. Select the `payment_intent` events and the `charge.dispute` events, press **Continue**, choose **Webhook endpoint**, and press **Continue** again.
5. Paste the Callback URL as the **Endpoint URL**, and create the destination.
6. On its page, press **Reveal secret** and copy the secret. It starts with `whsec_`.

### Which payment methods buyers see (optional)

Left empty, the checkout offers what your Stripe account's default configuration offers, which can include Klarna and other ways to pay later. To choose for yourself:

1. Open [Payment methods](https://dashboard.stripe.com/settings/payment_methods) in Stripe's settings.
2. In **Payment configurations**, open the menu (⋯), press **Create a configuration**, name it, switch on the methods you want, and press **Save configuration**.
3. Copy the configuration's ID. It starts with `pmc_`.

## In Shoply

1. In your Shoply dashboard, open **Settings**, then **Payment Methods**.
2. Under **Add a Payment Method**, find **Stripe** and press **Connect**.
3. Paste the secret key into **Secret Key** and the publishable key into **Publishable Key**.
4. If you made the webhook, paste its secret into **Webhook Signing Secret**. If you made a configuration, paste its ID into **Payment Method Configuration ID**.
5. Press **Test Connection** to try what you pasted, then **Connect App**.

**Test Connection** says whether the key is a live one. A sandbox key (`sk_test_`) works too: test cards complete and deliver orders, and no money moves. A key that does not start with `sk_` or `rk_` is refused.

How Stripe works once it is connected, at the checkout and on an order, is in [Payment methods](/guides/payment-methods).
