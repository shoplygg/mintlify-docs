Adyen takes cards, and the other payment methods you switch on in your merchant account, on its own payment page. You need an Adyen account: a test account to try it, and a live one to take real payments.

## In Adyen

### The API key

1. Log in to your Customer Area ([ca-test.adyen.com](https://ca-test.adyen.com) for a test account, [ca-live.adyen.com](https://ca-live.adyen.com) for a live one) and select your **Company** account.
2. Go to **Developers**, then **API credentials**, and select the **Payments** tab.
3. Select a credential's username. To make one instead, select **Create new credential**, choose **Web service user** and select **Create credential**.
4. Under **Server settings**, **Authentication**, select the **API key** tab, then **Generate API key**.
5. Copy the key with its copy icon: Adyen does not show it again once you leave the page. Then select **Save changes**.

### The merchant account

Its name is in the account switcher in the upper left of your Customer Area: select your company's name there to see your merchant accounts. Switched to it, check under **Payments**, **Payment methods** that it has at least one payment method. The ones you add there are the ones Adyen's page offers.

### The webhook

1. Open Shoply's Adyen dialog and copy the **Callback URL** it shows over **HMAC Key**.
2. In your Customer Area, go to **Developers**, then **Webhooks**. Select **Create new webhook**, then **Add** next to **Standard webhook**.
3. Under **General**, leave it **Enabled** and choose your merchant account. Under **Server configuration**, paste the Callback URL as its **URL**, and choose **JSON** as its **Method**.
4. Under **Security**, generate an **HMAC Key** and copy it.
5. Select **Save configuration**.

Refunds and disputes are in the Standard webhook by default: there is nothing to switch on for them.

### A live account

- Copy its live URL prefix from **Developers**, **API URLs**, **Prefix** in your live Customer Area. It looks like `1797a841fbb37ca7-AdyenDemo`.
- Before its first payment, add the address of your terms and conditions under **Payments**, **Payment link settings**, **Terms and conditions URL**. Adyen shows it on its payment page, and asks for it before a live link is made.

Adyen captures a payment as soon as it is authorised, unless your merchant account is set to capture by hand. Leave it so: Shoply delivers an order once Adyen says it is paid, and a payment captured by hand is yours to capture in Adyen.

## In Shoply

1. In your Shoply dashboard, open **Settings**, then **Payment Methods**.
2. Under **Add a payment method**, find **Adyen** and press **Connect**.
3. Paste the key into **API Key**, and your merchant account's name into **Merchant Account**.
4. For a live account, paste its prefix into **Live URL Prefix**. The whole live address is taken too. Leave it empty for a test account.
5. Paste the webhook's key into **HMAC Key**.
6. Press **Test Connection** to try what you pasted, then **Connect App**.

Saving asks Adyen which payment methods your merchant account has, which proves the key, the account and the address go together, and refuses an account with none. **Test Connection** says whether it is a test account and how many payment methods it has.

Back on your webhook in Adyen, **Test configuration** sends Shoply a test. If it fails, the **HMAC Key** in Shoply is not that webhook's.

A live account cannot be made a test one by emptying **Live URL Prefix**: disconnect it and connect it again.

How Adyen works once it is connected, at the checkout and on an order, is in [Payment methods](/guides/payment-methods#adyen).
