A **storefront** is a design of your store's site. A store can hold up to ten and has one of them **live**, which is the one buyers see. The others are drafts to work on. Find them under **Online Store**, then **Storefronts**.

## Themes

A storefront starts from a theme. There are four: **Simplistic**, light and plain; **Keystone**, dark, with pictures in your store's colour; **Emerald**, dark, with a first screen that glows in your store's colour and rows of categories and reviews that slide past by themselves; and **Vanguard**, dark, on a page ruled in faint lines of your store's colour, with each product's status on its card and a store page grouped by category. All four come in nine languages, and show prices in the buyer's own currency where your store has that switched on.

## The visual builder

**Customize** opens the builder. A site is pages, a page is sections, and a section holds elements: headings, products, reviews, pictures, your own HTML. Pick something on the page to change it. Changes are saved as you make them and reach buyers when you press **Publish**.

Every theme also has **Before and After**: two of your pictures, one over the other, with a line a buyer slides across to compare them. Add it from the builder's sections; until you give it your pictures it shows the theme's own pair.

## The code

**Edit Code** opens the storefront's files: its layout, pages, parts, stylesheet and script, as templates you can change. A template is text and cannot run anything on the server. A mistake in one is listed, and stops a publish until it is fixed.

## Moving a storefront

**Export** gives you a storefront as a ZIP of its real files: change them in any editor, zip them again, and **Import** them as a new draft. Nothing in an import is trusted: its design is rebuilt from the theme and every file is checked. A storefront somebody else made can still carry a script of theirs, so read one before you publish it.

## What is always there

Three things are Shoply's and stay on every store: the cookie notice, "Powered by Shoply", and "Report Abuse".

## The checkout

The checkout is not part of a storefront and is not edited in the builder. For a checkout that looks like your own site, use the [Checkout Kit](/guides/checkout-kit).
