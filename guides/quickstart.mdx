---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a store">
    Sign up at [dashboard.shoply.gg](https://dashboard.shoply.gg), with an email and password or with Google. You are asked a few things about the store: its name, its address and its currency.

    The store answers at once at `your-name.myshoply.gg`. An account holds up to five stores.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a product">
    Open **Catalogue**, then **Products**, and press **Add product**. A product needs a name and at least one **variant**: an option a buyer picks, with its own price, such as "1 month" and "1 year".

    On the **Delivery** tab, say what each variant sends when it is paid for. For license keys, paste them in, one a line. See [how delivery works](/guides/delivery).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect a way to be paid">
    Open **Payments** and connect a payment method: cards, PayPal or crypto. Buyers pay you directly through the account you connect. See [payment methods](/guides/payment-methods).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make a test sale">
    Open your store, add the product to the cart and check out. The checkout is at `checkout.shoply.gg`: the buyer gives their email, pays, and what they bought is on that page and in their inbox a moment later.

    The order is under **Orders** in the dashboard, with what was delivered.
  </Step>
</Steps>
