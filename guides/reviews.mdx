---
title: "Reviews"
description: "What buyers say about their orders: how a review is left, where it shows, and what nobody can do to one"
---

A review belongs to a paid order. Every order can have one: a rating from one to five stars, and words if the buyer has any, up to 1,000 characters.

## How one is left

| | |
| - | - |
| **By the buyer** | On the order's own page, once it is paid. Every receipt has a **Leave a review** button that leads there. That button is on every store's receipts and cannot be switched off. |
| **By itself** | An order nobody reviewed gets five stars seven days after it was paid, marked as left automatically. |

Automatic reviews are on for a new store. To stop them, open **Settings**, then **Statistics**, and switch **Automatic reviews** off. Orders already reviewed keep what they have.

## A review is final

Nobody changes a review once it is left.

- The buyer cannot edit theirs, or replace one that was left automatically.
- You cannot edit, hide or delete one, from the dashboard or with the API.

That is the point of them: a rating a store could prune would tell a buyer nothing.

## Where they show

Your store's **Reviews** page lists them, newest first, each with what was bought. A buyer is shown as a verified buyer and never by their email address. A product's rating and its number of reviews, where a theme shows them, are worked out from the reviews of orders that held it.

In the dashboard they are under **Audience**, then **Reviews**: the average rating, how many are five stars, how many were written by buyers and how many were left automatically. Search by a buyer's email, an order's ID or a review's words.

## Being told

Under **Settings**, then **Notifications**, **New reviews** posts each review a buyer writes to your store's Discord, with its stars, its words and its order. Automatic reviews are not posted.

Your own server can hear of every review, automatic ones included, with the [`review.created`](/api-reference/webhook-events#reviews) webhook event.

## With the API

Reviews can be read and nothing else: see [List reviews](/api-reference/reviews/list-reviews).
